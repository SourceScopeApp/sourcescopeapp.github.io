Questions before you install

Useful before you pay, clear about Pro, and careful with your research data.

Is SourceScope free?

Yes. Free scanning works without an account. You can check Source Health, view Source Map signals, preview source leads, and use 3 source recovery scans per day before upgrading.

What does Pro unlock?

Pro unlocks every source lead, unlimited recovery scans, more saves, projects, Source Packs, exports, Reference Integrity CSV, recovery reports, batch packs, and comparisons.

Is SourceScope a fact checker?

No. SourceScope does not decide whether an article is true or whether a source proves a claim. It gives you ratings, citation context, archive recovery, and export tools so you can inspect faster.

Where is my data stored?

Saved reports, sources, projects, and Source Packs are stored locally in your browser. User-triggered lookups may contact public metadata, archive, or page sources only when you use those features.

How do I cancel?

Paid users can use Manage Pro inside SourceScope to open the ExtensionPay payment flow. You can cancel before renewal to stop future billing.

Is there a refund policy?

Your first SourceScope Pro purchase is eligible for a refund within 7 days if SourceScope does not fit your workflow. See the refund policy for details.
